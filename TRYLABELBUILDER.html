Source-Preserving Label Mode
Label pages are now imported as native PDF pages—no canvas rasterization, auto-cropping, rotation, or rescaling—so vector artwork, embedded text, and barcode geometry are retained as closely as the PDF page-copy process allows. Label pages keep their original page dimensions; this takes priority over forcing them into a common page size. Source files are read-only and byte-signature checked. Manifest pages are embedded as native PDF page content and scaled to fit the stacking area without rasterizing or content-cropping. Scaling changes physical size, and the combined PDF is reserialized, so this is not a byte-for-byte copy of the original files.
